Ribeye Steak

A Riberye Steak my favorite food to eat

Ingredients

  • one ribeye steak
  • 4 cups of yoshidas teriyaki sauce
  • 2 tablespoons of Montreal steak seasoning
  • 2 tablespoons of Buttery steakhouse seasoning
  • 2 tablespoons of Maple bourbon seasoning
  • 2 tablespoons of Kinder's steak blend seasoning
  • 2 tablespoons of Smokehouse maple seasoning

Directions

  1. Put the seasonings and the teriyaki sauce in a glass pan and mix it together to make the marinade.
  2. Put the steak into the marinade.
  3. Flip the steak in the marinade every 30 minutes for 3 hours.
  4. Preheat the barbeque to 350 degrees fahrenheit.
  5. Put the steak on the barbeque.
  6. Flip the steak every 20 minutes untill you think its done enough for you.
  7. Once the steak is done take it of the barbeque and put it on a plate.
  8. Turn of the barbeque.
